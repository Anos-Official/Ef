The Boy Who

Presentation by Daniel

The Problem · 02

No Rain. No Food.

01
The Crisis
Drought killed the crops and cut off water, pushing the village into starvation
02
The Solution
William built a windmill from scrap to power a pump and restore the water supply
03
The Outcome
The village planted crops again and found a path back to survival

Entrepreneurial Characteristics · 03

What Made William Different

01
Innovation
He learned on his own using library books which helped him to make something people thought he couldn't.
02
Persistence
His friends left him, money was tight, yet he never stopped and kept going.
03
Resourcefulness
No money, no resources — everything he used was scavenged from a junkyard and wood from trees.

What Made Them Successful · 04

What Determined
The Outcome

What Helped
The fact that William did not once give up, sneaking into school to study.
Never giving up hope on the windmill, even though his dog died.
What Made It Harder
Firstly, his friends leaving him.
Then his dad not being willing to help, though he later changed and gave him his bike.
Overall, the lack of resources made it a lot more difficult.

Barriers & Challenges · 05

What Stood In His Way

The Obstacles
He faced a drought which prevented farming.
A lack of resources.
His father's disapproval and his friends leaving when trying to build the windmill.
How He Responded
He was as resourceful as possible getting materials from the junkyard to help.
When his dad's mindset changed due to a conversation with his mother, he also helped and gave him his bike.
Ultimately building a windmill and bringing water to start farming again.

Personal Connection · 06

If It Were Me

What I'd Do Differently
I feel like there could have been not a lot that I could have done differently. I might have tried a little harder to persuade his father to trust his idea, but I believe William tried his best to reach his goal.
Traits I Already Have
I believe I have the characteristic of persistence, especially shown in my persistence for "my offer" and belief in gambling being a way of life, though I don't partake in it.
Traits I Need to Improve
I believe I need to work on my initiative and communication skills.
